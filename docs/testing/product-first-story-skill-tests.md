# Product-first skill pressure record

Historical verbatim worker transcripts were removed because they documented superseded local
Product Contracts, delivery profiles, design gates, and legacy branches. Current RED/GREEN evidence
is maintained in `native-information-coordination-pressure-tests.md`; concise acceptance cases are
in `product-first-story-smoke-tests.md` and `dual-runtime-smoke-tests.md`.

The retained evidence boundary is deliberate: record scenarios, source categories, observed
decisions, and verdicts. Do not retain raw conversations, connector payloads, opaque external IDs,
or chronological delivery logs.
