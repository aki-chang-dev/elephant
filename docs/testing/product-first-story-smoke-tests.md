# Native product-story smoke tests

## One sentence to one approval

Start with a one-sentence user-visible idea. `shape-story` must fetch the current Linear Story and
directly linked Notion meaning, discuss user outcome, experience, copy, feedback, recovery, rules,
and observable acceptance, then show one complete recap. Approval authorizes only the displayed
Linear and Notion changes; technical design begins after verified read-back.

## Progressive planning structure

Exercise three ideas: a standalone Story, a Story related directly to an Objective, and a genuine
multi-phase workstream. Elephant must respectively keep the Story standalone; show `Related
Objective` without synthetic Project or Objective progress; and introduce only the useful
Project/Milestone/Initiative levels discovered during shaping.

## Knowledge disposition

Exercise `linear_only`, `decision`, `knowledge`, and `decision_and_knowledge`. Notion receives only
durable product meaning whose future retrieval changes understanding or action. Raw discussion,
routine progress, plans, delivery logs, and copied Linear fields must not be persisted there.

## Routed retrieval

Ask one planning/status question, one product-meaning question, and one executable-behavior
question. The first read must respectively use the mapped Linear Product scope, mapped Notion
Product Home/Knowledge Map, and repository. Direct relations are followed before any scoped
expansion; a valid workspace anchor must short-circuit global search.

## Technical source binding

Technical design must fetch complete current Linear and linked Notion source bodies and map every
observable requirement. A changed observable source invalidates downstream design, plan,
implementation, and verdicts. Pure engineering disagreement stays with specialist agents; changed
product meaning returns to bounded shaping.

## Native delivery linkage

`ship-story CF-123` must place `CF-123` in branch and pull-request identity, verify the native
Linear-GitHub relation, and otherwise write one ordinary fallback link. Factual status and risks
are updated automatically; owner attention is requested only for outcome, acceptance, ownership,
or strategic-priority changes. Transient Technical Contract and plan files are removed before
integration.

## Interrupted native write

After one approved recap, interrupt an external create or append. Resume must retain the approved
meaning, read the exact native scope, distinguish zero/one/multiple matches, and retry at most once
after connector health plus two fresh reads. It must not ask the owner to reapprove unchanged
product decisions.
